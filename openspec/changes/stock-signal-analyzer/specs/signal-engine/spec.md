## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Signal confidence calculation

The system SHALL compute a confidence value (0.0 to 1.0) for each of the 11 signals, based on market data obtained from the `pipeline/data/` layer.

The 11 signals SHALL be:
- **Left-side (6):** vol_shrink (缩量下跌), no_new_low (跌不动), false_breakdown (假破位收回), vol_contraction (波动收敛), chip_concentration (筹码集中), market_env (大盘环境)
- **Right-side (5):** above_ma (站回均线), support_retest_hold (回踩不破), volume_breakout (放量反包), macd_cross (MACD金叉), higher_low (低点抬升)

Each confidence value SHALL have a physically interpretable formula tied to observable market data.

#### Scenario: Compute vol_shrink confidence
- **WHEN** analyzer calculates signal "vol_shrink" for ticker "AAPL"
- **THEN** confidence = clamp((1 - vol5/vol20) * 2.0, 0, 1) where vol5 is 5-day average volume and vol20 is 20-day average volume

#### Scenario: Compute above_ma confidence
- **WHEN** analyzer calculates signal "above_ma" and close > MA20
- **THEN** confidence = clamp((close - MA20) / ATR, 0, 1)

#### Scenario: Signal when close is below MA20
- **WHEN** analyzer calculates signal "above_ma" and close < MA20
- **THEN** confidence = 0.0 and light = "red"

#### Scenario: Compute support_retest_hold confidence
- **WHEN** analyzer detects a false breakdown reclaim and price later retests the reclaimed support zone without breaking below it
- **THEN** signal "support_retest_hold" confidence is based on touch proximity, close-above-support strength, low-above-support strength, and support quality

### Requirement: Signal light mapping

Each signal SHALL map its confidence to a three-state light based on signal-specific thresholds.

Default thresholds SHALL be: red [0, 0.35), yellow [0.35, 0.70), green [0.70, 1.0].

Signals with custom thresholds:
- false_breakdown: green ≥ 0.60
- volume_breakout: green ≥ 0.75

#### Scenario: High confidence maps to green
- **WHEN** signal confidence is 0.85 and thresholds are default
- **THEN** light = "green"

#### Scenario: Mid confidence maps to yellow
- **WHEN** signal confidence is 0.52 and thresholds are default
- **THEN** light = "yellow"

#### Scenario: Low confidence maps to red
- **WHEN** signal confidence is 0.20 and thresholds are default
- **THEN** light = "red"

### Requirement: Phase determination

The system SHALL determine the stock's current phase based on the count of green-lit signals in left-side and right-side categories.

Phase rules SHALL be:
- left_green ≤ 1 → "仍在下跌" (🔴)
- left_green ≤ 3 AND right_green == 0 → "底部特征初现" (🟡)
- left_green ≥ 4 AND right_green ≤ 1 → "底部基本成型" (🟡⭐)
- left_green ≥ 3 AND right_green ≥ 2 → "右侧初步确认" (🟢)
- left_green ≥ 4 AND right_green ≥ 3 → "趋势已确立" (🟢🟢)

Rules SHALL be evaluated top-to-bottom; the last matching rule wins.

#### Scenario: Stock in downtrend
- **WHEN** left_green = 1 and right_green = 0
- **THEN** phase = "仍在下跌" with icon 🔴

#### Scenario: Bottom forming
- **WHEN** left_green = 5 and right_green = 1
- **THEN** phase = "底部基本成型" with icon 🟡⭐

#### Scenario: Right side confirmed
- **WHEN** left_green = 4 and right_green = 3
- **THEN** phase = "趋势已确立" with icon 🟢🟢

### Requirement: Overall strength calculation

The system SHALL compute an overall strength score as the weighted average of all signal confidences.

Formula: `strength = sum(signal.confidence * signal.weight) / sum(signal.weight)`

Signal weights: false_breakdown = 2, support_retest_hold = 2, volume_breakout = 2, all others = 1.

The strength value SHALL be displayed with a range bar showing phase boundaries: [🔴 0-25% │ 🟡 25-45% │ 🟡⭐ 45-60% │ 🟢 60-80% │ 🟢🟢 80%+]

#### Scenario: Calculate overall strength
- **WHEN** all 10 signals have been computed with their confidences and weights
- **THEN** overall strength = weighted average, displayed as percentage with range bar

### Requirement: Action recommendation

Based on the determined phase, the system SHALL output a specific action recommendation.

Mapping:
- 仍在下跌 → "不碰，等待底部信号出现"
- 底部特征初现 → "列入观察清单"
- 底部基本成型 → "高度关注，等待右侧确认"
- 右侧初步确认 → "可考虑建仓"
- 趋势已确立 → "持有或加仓"

Additionally, the system SHALL output a "trigger condition" — the most likely next signal to flip green, phrased as a concrete price/volume level.

#### Scenario: Trigger condition for bottom forming phase
- **WHEN** phase = "底部基本成型" and above_ma is yellow (close near MA20)
- **THEN** trigger = "放量站上 MA20 ($XXX) 则确认右侧" where $XXX is the actual MA20 value

### Requirement: Narrative summary generation

The system SHALL generate a narrative summary paragraph (2-4 sentences) describing the analysis conclusion.

The narrative SHALL be generated by template concatenation (not LLM), using pre-defined sentence fragments selected based on signal states.

The narrative SHALL mention: current phase, strongest left-side signals, right-side signal status, and what to watch for next.

#### Scenario: Generate narrative for bottom forming
- **WHEN** phase = "底部基本成型" with vol_shrink green, no_new_low green, above_ma yellow
- **THEN** narrative includes mention of volume shrinkage, holding above previous low, and waiting for MA confirmation
